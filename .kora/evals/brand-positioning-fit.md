# Eval: Brand Positioning Fit

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a Marcos Dev page, offer, proposal, or content item fits the intended brand and positioning.

## Criteria

- The audience and problem are clear.
- The offer is specific and understandable.
- Claims are realistic and evidence-aware.
- Tone is direct, didactic, professional, and human.
- Visual and verbal identity are consistent with local guidance.
- The next action is obvious.

## Result Format

- Pass / Warning / Fail
- Evidence
- Recommended fix
