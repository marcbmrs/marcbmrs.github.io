# DR-0009: CanteiroPro Product Direction

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: Proposed working direction

## Context

Marcos wants to structure the Renan Reformas panel as a product opportunity inside the Marcos Dev repository, including name, positioning, KORA agents, skills, knowledge, and future promotion through Marcos Dev.

The Renan panel already proves a practical flow for small renovation providers: budget creation, saved quotes, status control, PDF, contract draft, work tracking, private client updates, photos, and WhatsApp-based communication.

External market signal: IBGE PAIC 2024 reported a large construction market with 191 thousand companies, 2.5 million employed people, and R$ 522.5 billion generated in works, incorporations, and specialized services.

## Decision

Use `CanteiroPro` as the working product name and structure it first as a Marcos Dev productized service, not as a self-serve SaaS.

## Rationale

- The current system is strongest as a configured one-client installation.
- The audience likely values practical setup and support more than complex software self-service.
- A productized service reduces risk while Marcos validates demand.
- The Renan panel can become a reference implementation without exposing private client data.

## Scope

Initial offer:

- setup and configuration;
- service catalog adaptation;
- budget workflow;
- PDF;
- contract draft workflow with legal caveat;
- work tracking;
- client private page;
- support/hosting.

Out of initial scope:

- multi-tenant SaaS;
- electronic signature;
- accounting/tax automation;
- payment processing;
- legal guarantee for contracts;
- public use of real Renan/client data without approval.

## Consequences

- Create a neutral demo before public sales.
- Build a Marcos Dev offer/landing page around the pain and flow.
- Use KORA product, growth, offer, and demo capabilities for future decisions.
- Validate pricing through discovery conversations and first paid pilots.
