# Skill: Plan Instagram Content From Strategy

## Purpose

Create Marcos Dev Instagram content ideas, Reels/video scripts, carousels, Stories, captions, hooks, and editorial variations from strategy, evidence, and recent post history.

Use this skill when Marcos asks for ideas for posts, videos, Reels, carousels, Stories, storytelling angles, content calendar items, hooks, captions, or social media methodology for Marcos Dev.

## Core Context

Marcos Dev helps professionals and small businesses reduce friction in digital presence and operations. Current priority audience includes local service businesses, especially home/construction-related niches such as renovations, planned furniture, architecture, and nearby local businesses in Viamão/Porto Alegre region.

Main content should help the audience understand problems such as:

- difficulty being found;
- unclear explanation of services;
- low trust before first contact;
- weak portfolio/proof presentation;
- confusing contact path;
- operational friction that may need site, landing page, Google local presence, automation, or system work.

Tone: human, direct, didactic, practical, calm, and ethical. Avoid generic hype, false urgency, humiliation of competitors, promises of ranking, leads, sales, virality, or guaranteed results.

## Workflow

1. Identify the content objective: discovery, explanation, trust, proof, conversation, offer, relationship, or learning.
2. Identify the audience/context: owner, professional, local service business, construction/home niche, or another explicit segment.
3. Check recent post history when available, especially theme, angle, format, hook, CTA, and repeated thesis.
4. Choose the format based on the idea:
   - Reel/video for a concrete scene, contrast, quick explanation, or behind-the-scenes reasoning.
   - Carousel for step-by-step education, before/after logic, checklist, or objection handling.
   - Static post for proof, announcement, quote, or focused idea.
   - Stories for informal progress, poll, reminder, backstage, or low-friction interaction.
5. Build the idea with one clear promise of usefulness, one narrative movement, and one proportional next action.
6. When using storytelling, prefer concrete micro-stories: a person trying to choose, a business losing clarity before contact, a project decision, a customer doubt, a before/after of information, or a small operational problem becoming visible.
7. Produce only what was asked: idea list, content calendar, script, carousel structure, caption, visual briefing, or variations.
8. Mark facts, hypotheses, and recommendations separately when discussing expected performance.
9. Under KORA level 7, persist only approved durable editorial directions or repeated learning patterns, not every brainstorm.

## Storytelling Patterns

Use these patterns when helpful, not mechanically:

- Before contact: show what the potential client needs to understand before sending a WhatsApp message.
- Hidden friction: reveal a small obstacle that makes a good business seem harder to hire.
- Project reasoning: explain a decision made in a real site/project and why it matters.
- Contrast: compare “more visibility” with “clearer first impression”.
- Small diagnostic: ask one concrete question the business owner can answer by looking at their profile/site.
- Proof with limits: show real work or process without inventing results.

## Output Shapes

For idea requests, return each idea with:

- format;
- objective;
- hook;
- core story/angle;
- structure;
- CTA;
- why it fits Marcos Dev;
- risk or care point.

For Reel/video scripts, include:

- opening hook;
- scene or visual direction;
- voiceover or talking points;
- on-screen text;
- caption;
- CTA;
- optional variation.

For carousels, include:

- slide-by-slide outline;
- cover text;
- key idea per slide;
- final CTA;
- caption;
- visual direction.

## Boundaries

Do not publish, schedule, send Directs, reply to comments, or claim the content will perform.

Do not use private client data, raw credentials, screenshots, or project details unless Marcos explicitly provides and authorizes them for that content.

Do not create content that implies professional/legal/medical/financial claims outside Marcos Dev's scope.

For third-party profile analysis, use only public evidence supplied by Marcos and do not use Marcos Dev Instagram credentials.
