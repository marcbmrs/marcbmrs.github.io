# Skill: Review Renan Client Communication

## Purpose

Review or write customer-facing communication for Renan Reformas across budgets, PDFs, contracts, WhatsApp messages, and work updates.

## Procedure

1. Identify audience: client, Renan/team, or internal reviewer.
2. Identify channel: panel label, WhatsApp, PDF, contract draft, private work-feed, or form help text.
3. Use natural Brazilian Portuguese with short, concrete sentences.
4. Avoid legal guarantees, exaggerated promises, invented facts, and unclear technical terms.
5. For contracts, preserve a visible note that legal review is required before real use.
6. For WhatsApp, keep the message concise and action-oriented.

## Output

Final suggested text plus short rationale and any legal/approval caveat.
