# Eval: CanteiroPro Offer Quality

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a CanteiroPro offer, landing page, pitch, or proposal is clear, believable, and useful for Marcos Dev prospects.

## Criteria

- Target audience is specific enough.
- Pain is concrete and recognizable.
- Promise is simple and not exaggerated.
- Deliverables are explicit.
- Exclusions are clear for legal, accounting, signature, payments, and full SaaS features.
- CTA matches the buyer stage.
- Proof does not reveal private Renan/client data.
- Copy sounds like Marcos Dev: clear, human, organized, practical, and trustworthy.

## Result Format

- Pass / Warning / Fail
- Audience fit
- Promise clarity
- Scope clarity
- Proof safety
- CTA quality
- Required changes
