# Skill: Review Offer and Positioning

## Purpose

Turn a Marcos Dev service, page, proposal, or content idea into a clear offer and positioning check.

## Procedure

1. Identify the target audience and context.
2. State the promise in plain language without exaggeration.
3. Confirm the deliverables, boundaries, prerequisites, and likely objections.
4. Check whether the CTA matches the stage: learn, diagnose, call, request proposal, or start project.
5. Separate known facts from assumptions that need user approval or evidence.
6. Suggest the smallest next improvement to copy, offer, proof, or flow.

## Output

Offer/positioning review with recommended wording, risks, and open questions.
