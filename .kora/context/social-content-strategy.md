# Marcos Dev Social Content Strategy

## Purpose

Local KORA context for Marcos Dev Instagram and social media decisions, independent from MarcosOS.

## Business Role Of Content

Instagram content should help Marcos Dev attract and educate professionals and small businesses by making digital-presence problems easier to notice and discuss.

Content should support:

- discovery by qualified local/small-business audiences;
- understanding of what Marcos Dev solves;
- trust through real work, reasoning, and clear limits;
- conversations through proportional CTAs;
- learning about which themes and offers deserve repetition.

## Priority Audience

Primary initial audience:

- local service businesses;
- home and construction-related niches;
- renovations, planned furniture, architecture, trades, and nearby local businesses;
- Viamão and Porto Alegre metropolitan context when relevant, without making geography a hard limit.

Common audience questions:

- What does this business actually do?
- Can I trust it before contacting?
- Can I see real work or examples?
- Is the next step clear?
- Does a site, Google profile, landing page, automation, or system solve my current problem?

## Content Pillars

- Real projects: show what was organized and why it matters.
- First impression: explain what a potential client understands before contacting.
- Local presence: Google profile, site, Instagram, portfolio, reviews, region, and contact clarity.
- Behind the decision: explain design, UX, copy, structure, or technical choices in simple language.
- Small diagnostics: one useful question or check a business owner can apply immediately.
- Offers with limits: explain when a service makes sense and when it does not.
- Responsible growth: avoid selling a bigger solution when a smaller fix is enough.

## Preferred Angles

- A good business can lose contacts before the first message if the digital path is unclear.
- More traffic does not fix an unclear first impression.
- The site is not the business; it organizes trust, information, and next action.
- A project is stronger when it explains what the client needs to understand, not only when it looks polished.
- Simple, specific improvements can reduce friction without promising sales or ranking.

## Content Rules

- Prefer concrete examples over abstract advice.
- Do not invent client results, metrics, testimonials, or proof.
- Do not promise leads, ranking, sales, virality, or algorithmic advantage.
- Do not use fear, fake urgency, engagement bait, competitor humiliation, or mass Direct logic.
- Separate fact, hypothesis, and recommendation when interpreting performance.
- Treat likes and comments as weak signals unless connected to qualified conversations or official Insights.

## CTA Patterns

Use CTAs that match the content commitment level:

- low commitment: "olhe a primeira seção do seu site e veja se isso aparece claro";
- conversation: "me envie seu site ou Instagram para eu apontar o primeiro atrito";
- offer entry: "peça uma Primeira Impressão Digital";
- project inquiry: "me chame para organizar a presença digital do seu negócio".

Avoid CTAs that imply guaranteed diagnosis, guaranteed sales, automated audit, or pressure.

## Current Learning

As of 2026-09-05, posts showing real projects with reasoning appear strategically stronger than purely conceptual carousels, based on available public interaction signals. This is a hypothesis, not a final performance conclusion, because official Insights such as reach, saves, shares, profile visits, and DMs were not available in the API read.
