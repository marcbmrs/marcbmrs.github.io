# Skill: Review Landing Page Change

## Purpose

Evaluate a Marcos Dev landing page or campaign page change before considering it ready.

## Procedure

1. Identify the page, route, owner, and intended business outcome.
2. Confirm whether it is the official Marcos Dev site, a service page, a client project, a demo, or a campaign.
3. Review message hierarchy: audience, problem, offer, proof, objections, CTA, and contact path.
4. Review implementation: responsive layout, links, images, metadata, accessibility, performance, and URL stability.
5. Check repository boundaries: no private assets, secrets, unpublished client data, or accidental root-site changes.
6. Under level 7, record durable decisions, formal eval results, or recurring gaps when they are useful for future Marcos Dev work and safe to persist locally.

## Output

Landing page review with status: ready, ready with notes, or not ready.
