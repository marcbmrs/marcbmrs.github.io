## Boundaries

Do not copy KORA Core into this repository.

Do not store full Marcos Dev strategy inside KORA Core.

Do not store credentials, tokens, private account access, or unnecessary personal/client data in `.kora/`.

Project-specific agents, skills, tools, evals, automations, experiments, memory, and decisions should stay local unless KORA explicitly classifies them as reusable.

MarcosOS is now treated as a legacy/transitional knowledge source for Marcos Dev. KORA local should progressively absorb only the project-specific context, procedures, tools, evals, and decisions needed for Marcos Dev to operate without MarcosOS. Do not copy the whole MarcosOS or migrate generic reusable knowledge without scope analysis.
