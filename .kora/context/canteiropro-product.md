# CanteiroPro Product Context

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: working product direction

## Product Definition

CanteiroPro is the working name for a Marcos Dev productized service based on the Renan Reformas panel.

It is a configured operational panel for small construction and field-service providers who need to organize quotes, approvals, contract drafts, work progress, photos, and client communication.

## Positioning

Primary positioning:

> Um painel simples para organizar orçamentos, contratos e atualizações de obra, com aparência profissional para o cliente.

Alternative short positioning:

- O painel de obra para quem vende, executa e precisa manter o cliente informado.
- Do orçamento à atualização da obra, tudo em um painel simples.
- Menos orçamento perdido no WhatsApp. Mais obra organizada.

## Audience

Initial audience:

- pedreiros;
- empreiteiros pequenos;
- equipes de reforma;
- pintores;
- gesseiros and drywall installers;
- eletricistas;
- encanadores;
- telhadistas;
- colocadores de piso/revestimento;
- manutenção predial pequena.

Best-fit customer profile:

- works mostly through WhatsApp;
- sends informal budgets or PDFs manually;
- handles more than one client/job at a time;
- wants to look more professional without learning a complex ERP;
- values mobile-first use more than desktop-heavy back office features.

## Core Pain

The customer loses time and clarity because budget details, approvals, photos, contract notes, and client updates are scattered across WhatsApp, notebooks, galleries, and memory.

## Core Offer

Configured setup plus monthly support/hosting.

Likely package:

- business identity setup;
- service catalog adaptation;
- budget flow;
- PDF layout;
- contract draft model;
- work tracking;
- client private page;
- basic training;
- maintenance/support.

## Product Stage

Do not treat as validated SaaS yet.

Recommended stage:

1. Productized service.
2. Repeatable installation.
3. Demo/sales page.
4. Paid pilots.
5. Only then consider multi-tenant SaaS.

## Market Evidence

The construction market is large and fragmented, which supports a service for smaller operators. The IBGE PAIC 2024 news release reported 191 thousand construction companies, 2.5 million employed people, and R$ 522.5 billion in works, incorporations, and specialized services.

Source: https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/47043-em-2024-industria-da-construcao-gera-r-522-5-bilhoes-e-emprega-2-5-milhoes

## Risks

- The Renan panel still contains client-specific brand and copy.
- Current architecture is better for one installation per client than for multi-tenant SaaS.
- Contract features need legal framing and review.
- Client photos and private work updates require explicit privacy/security handling.
- Pricing model still needs validation with real prospects.

## Next Moves

- Extract configurable business settings from Renan-specific strings.
- Define offer packages and pricing hypothesis.
- Create Marcos Dev landing section/page.
- Create first sales content sequence for Instagram and WhatsApp.
- Run 3 to 5 discovery conversations with target professionals.
