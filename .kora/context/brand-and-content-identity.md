# Marcos Dev Brand And Content Identity

## Purpose

Local KORA context for Marcos Dev brand voice and visual direction used in site and social content work.

## Brand Direction

Marcos Dev should feel:

- clear;
- human;
- organized;
- practical;
- trustworthy;
- technologically competent without sounding cold or generic.

## Visual Identity

Current working identity:

- primary navy: `#0B1F3A`;
- blue accent: `#2563EB`;
- aqua/green accent: `#14B8A6`;
- light background: `#F8FAFC`;
- titles: Manrope when available;
- supporting text: Inter when available.

Use visual hierarchy, spacing, contrast, and mobile legibility before decorative effects.

## Illustration Direction

When a content piece needs illustration, prefer mature editorial illustration with subtle cel shading, clean shapes, restrained light, professional context, and objects that help the narrative.

For business/local-service contexts, represent real work situations: desk, budget, catalog, phone, workshop, construction detail, reception, samples, or service environment.

Do not use generic luxury office visuals, childish style, fantasy, copied third-party styles, or decoration that does not support the idea.

Use real authorized photos when proof of project, product, client, work, or environment matters.

## Voice

Write in a natural, direct, didactic way.

Good content sounds like a person explaining a useful observation, not a campaign shouting for attention.

Prefer:

- short concrete examples;
- honest limits;
- practical next steps;
- one idea at a time;
- language a small-business owner can understand.

Avoid:

- exaggerated promises;
- jargon-heavy marketing;
- vague motivational posts;
- pressure language;
- pretending to have measured what was not measured.
