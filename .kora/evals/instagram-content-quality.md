# Eval: Instagram Content Quality

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a Marcos Dev Instagram idea, script, carousel, Story, or caption is strategically useful, clear, ethical, and ready for review.

## Criteria

- Objective is explicit: discovery, explanation, trust, proof, conversation, offer, relationship, or learning.
- Audience and situation are clear enough for the content to feel specific.
- Hook is honest, concrete, and understandable in a mobile feed.
- Story or structure has a clear movement instead of a loose list of claims.
- CTA is proportional to the content and does not over-ask.
- Content connects naturally to a real Marcos Dev capability or point of view.
- It does not repeat a recent theme, angle, format, hook, or CTA without adding a new useful layer.
- It avoids promises of ranking, leads, sales, virality, algorithmic advantage, or guaranteed results.
- It does not use fear, false urgency, engagement bait, mass outreach, or competitor humiliation.
- Visual/copy direction supports legibility, hierarchy, brand fit, and mobile comprehension.
- Any fact, proof, client/project detail, or platform claim is either verified, explicitly provided, or marked as a hypothesis/placeholder.

## Result Format

- Pass / Warning / Fail
- Content reviewed
- Strongest element
- Main risk
- Required correction
- Optional improvement
