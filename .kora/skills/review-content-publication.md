# Skill: Review Content Publication

## Purpose

Review Marcos Dev content before it is published or packaged for a channel.

## Procedure

1. Identify the channel: site, blog-like page, Instagram, diagnostic, proposal, campaign, or client material.
2. Confirm audience, objective, core message, and desired action.
3. Check factual accuracy, tone, clarity, and reuse of approved brand/business context.
4. Verify that no client-sensitive, private, credential, account, or unpublished information is exposed.
5. For Instagram-related production, follow local KORA instructions first; use MarcosOS instructions only as transitional legacy context while available.
6. Define what still needs human approval before publication.

## Output

Publication readiness note with approved, needs edits, or blocked.
