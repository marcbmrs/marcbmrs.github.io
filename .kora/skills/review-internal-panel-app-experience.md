# Skill: Review Internal Panel App Experience

## Purpose

Review internal panels for mobile-first operational UX and native-app feel, especially budget, contract, work-tracking, and client-update workflows.

## Procedure

1. Identify the panel folder, primary users, and top workflows.
2. Read the project README, panel context, main PHP views, shared CSS, and relevant scripts.
3. Evaluate the flow as a mobile app:
   - dashboard and task resumption;
   - bottom navigation and active state;
   - creation/editing forms;
   - lists, filters, search, empty states, and loading states;
   - detail pages and status transitions;
   - modals, confirmations, save states, and error recovery;
   - device affordances such as camera, WhatsApp, clipboard, and share links;
   - accessibility, touch targets, readable density, and keyboard/focus behavior.
4. Separate recommendations into quick wins, structural improvements, and future product features.
5. Flag any item that requires database changes, production credentials, private client media, legal review, or deployment approval.

## Output

A concise app-experience review:

- overall assessment;
- prioritized recommendations;
- suggested implementation sequence;
- capability gaps worth persisting in KORA.
