# Experiment: CanteiroPro Pricing Hypothesis

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: working hypothesis

## Context

Marcos proposed pricing CanteiroPro as a productized Marcos Dev offer:

- implementation: R$ 1.000 to R$ 1.500;
- monthly maintenance/support: R$ 200 to R$ 250;
- included in the commercial package: landing page and operational system;
- domain and hosting costs should be charged separately or passed through transparently, especially in pilot offers.

The product remains local-only until a sale or explicit deployment approval.

## Hypothesis

The proposed pricing can work for the first pilots if positioned as a configured operational package, not as generic software subscription.

## Offer Shape

Recommended first version:

- setup fee covers configuration, landing page, domain/hospedagem setup labor, initial service catalog adaptation, first training, and production installation;
- domain registration and hosting plan are external costs charged separately or reimbursed by the client;
- monthly fee covers light support, backups/checks, small adjustments, and keeping the system maintained;
- custom changes outside the agreed panel scope should be quoted separately.

## Validation Needed

- Confirm what domain cost is included and whether renewal is passed through or embedded.
- Define support limit per month to avoid unlimited work.
- Define what counts as "small adjustment" versus paid customization.
- Test pricing in 3 to 5 conversations with pedreiros, small contractors, renovation teams, painters, electricians, plumbers, and finishing specialists.
- Compare against cheaper SaaS options by emphasizing configuration, local support, landing page, and done-for-you setup.

## Pricing Risks

- Monthly price may feel expensive for informal solo providers unless the value is tied to more professional quotes and client confidence.
- Implementation price may be too low if each client needs heavy customization.
- Including domain, hosting, landing page, and system under one price requires clear boundaries.

## Current Recommendation

Use three pilot packages:

- Start: R$ 997 setup + R$ 197/month + domain/hosting costs;
- Pro: R$ 1.497 setup + R$ 247/month + domain/hosting costs;
- Custom: from R$ 2.500 setup + from R$ 350/month + infrastructure costs.

Treat these as market-test anchors, not final pricing.
