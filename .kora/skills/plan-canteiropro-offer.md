# Skill: Plan CanteiroPro Offer

## Purpose

Turn CanteiroPro into a clear Marcos Dev offer before writing sales pages, proposals, or social content.

## Procedure

1. Identify the target trade or service segment.
2. State the pain in the customer's language.
3. Choose the offer stage: discovery, demo, pilot, setup, monthly support, or custom implementation.
4. Define included deliverables and excluded promises.
5. Choose proof: Renan-derived flow, neutral demo, screenshots, or pilot result.
6. Define CTA appropriate to the stage.
7. List unanswered assumptions and validation needed.

## Output

Offer brief with audience, promise, deliverables, exclusions, proof, CTA, and validation questions.
