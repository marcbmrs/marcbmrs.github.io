# Overview

Marcos Dev is a business and operational website repository connected to KORA.

## Current Known Purpose

Marcos Dev offers digital services such as:

- website creation;
- system creation;
- digital presence;
- campaign pages;
- digital presence diagnostics;
- Instagram content support;
- digital projects for clients.

The root `index.html` is the official institutional site. Client projects, demos, campaigns, and reusable work should live under `projetos/<nome-do-projeto>/` unless the local `AGENTS.md` says otherwise.

## External Knowledge Boundary

MarcosOS, while accessible at `C:\MarcosOS`, is a legacy/transitional source for strategy, business, design, marketing, and reusable decision context. KORA in this repository should progressively hold the Marcos Dev-specific context needed to operate without MarcosOS.

## Local KORA Role

The local KORA layer should help future agents decide:

- when a change affects the official Marcos Dev site;
- when a client/project page needs its own review path;
- when offer, positioning, content, UX, SEO, accessibility, performance, or publishing quality should be checked;
- when a recurring decision should become a local agent, skill, eval, tool, or automation.
